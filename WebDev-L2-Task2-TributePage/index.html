About Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur, inventor, and business leader who played a major role in the development of personal technology. He was born on February 24, 1955, in San Francisco, California.

In 1976, Jobs co-founded Apple with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne. The company began by developing personal computers and eventually became one of the most influential technology companies in the world.

Jobs was known for his strong focus on design, simplicity, and innovation. During his career, he helped introduce products such as the Macintosh, iPod, iPhone, and iPad.

Steve Jobs died on October 5, 2011. His work continues to influence technology, design, business, and popular culture around the world.
